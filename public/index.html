FAQ

Your questions,
answered.

I have a high-end PC. Do I actually need this?+

Yes. Even the best hardware is often "choked" by default Windows settings designed for office work, not gaming. Hypertune focuses on consistency and 0.1% lows. We eliminate the micro-stutters and input lag that high-end parts still suffer from, ensuring your hardware hits its absolute ceiling.

Is this a subscription or a one-time purchase?+

Hypertune is a subscription service. We opted for this model because PC optimization isn't a 'set it and forget it' task. Windows updates and game patches launch weekly; our team works around the clock to update our logic so your PC stays optimized for the latest software.

What if I don't see an improvement?+

Every system is unique. If you don't see a measurable lift in FPS or a decrease in latency, reach out to our support team. We offer a 7-day free trial so you can see the results for yourself before ever being charged.

Will Hypertune get me banned (Valorant, Warzone, etc.)?+

No. Hypertune operates at the system and hardware level, not the game-file level. We do not modify game code or inject any files into your game directory. It is 100% anti-cheat safe and used by Tier-1 pros.

Can this break my Windows installation?+

Unlike manual YouTube registry tweaks that can be risky, Hypertune uses proprietary logic built by the performance specialists at Compready. Our optimizations are non-destructive and can be reverted with a single click.

Will the software slow my PC down while it's running?+

Quite the opposite. Hypertune is designed to be ultra-lightweight. Once your optimizations are applied, the app consumes near-zero resources, leaving all your CPU and RAM power for your games.

Does Hypertune overclock my hardware? Will it overheat?+

We offer optional Smart Overclocking for GPUs and Intel CPUs. Unlike "blind" boosts, our engine runs automated stress tests on your specific silicon to find the fastest stable speed. If your cooling can't handle it, Hypertune won't apply it.
